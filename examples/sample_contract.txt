FREELANCE SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Acme Tech Labs ("Client") and the individual
contractor ("Contractor").

1. Term and Renewal
The initial term is twelve (12) months. This Agreement shall automatically renew
for successive twelve-month periods unless the Contractor provides written notice
of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
Early termination by the Contractor is subject to an early termination fee equal
to two (2) months of the average monthly fee.

2. Fees and Payment
Invoices are due net 45 days. The Client may withhold payment pending its internal
review. All sales are final and fees are non-refundable. A late charge of 1.5%
per month applies to overdue amounts.

3. Intellectual Property
All rights in the work product, including all intellectual property and any
improvements, shall vest exclusively in the Client. The Contractor assigns all
intellectual property rights in anything created during the engagement, including
pre-existing tools the Contractor uses to deliver the work.

4. Liability
In no event shall the Client be liable for any indirect, consequential, or
incidental damages. The aggregate liability of the Client shall not exceed the
fees paid in the prior three (3) months. The Contractor shall indemnify and hold
harmless the Client from any claims arising out of the engagement, including
claims the Contractor did not directly cause.

5. Personal Guarantee
The Contractor personally guarantees payment of any amounts owed and is jointly
and severally liable for breaches of this Agreement.

6. Dispute Resolution
Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration under the rules of a provider
chosen by the Client, and the Contractor waives any right to a jury trial and to
participate in a class action. The governing law is the law of a jurisdiction
three time zones away from the Contractor's residence, and the venue shall be the
courts located there.

7. Changes
The Client may change the terms of this Agreement at its sole discretion, without
prior notice, and the Contractor's continued work constitutes acceptance.

8. Data
The Contractor consents to the Client collecting and sharing the Contractor's
personal data with third parties for any business purpose.

9. Non-Compete
The Contractor shall not engage with any client of the Client for twenty-four (24)
months following termination, worldwide and without geographic limit.

This Agreement is accepted by the Contractor's continued performance.
